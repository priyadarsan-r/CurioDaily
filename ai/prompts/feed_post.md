# Single-Image Feed Post Prompt Template

## Format Requirements
- One bold, high-contrast headline (under 12 words).
- One clear, digestible core insight (20-35 words).
- One or two supporting facts or quantitative stats.
- Clean caption with:
  - 2-3 paragraph breakdown.
  - Source attribution note.
  - 4-6 relevant educational hashtags (e.g. #curiodaily #knowledge #science).
