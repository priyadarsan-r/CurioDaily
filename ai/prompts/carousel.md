# Carousel Post Prompt Template (5 to 8 Slides)

## Format Requirements
- Slide 1 (Cover): Bold curiosity gap question or promise with clear subtitle.
- Slide 2 (Context): Why this matters and sets the stage.
- Slide 3 (Insight 1): First key mechanism or data point.
- Slide 4 (Insight 2): Second key mechanism or real-world comparison.
- Slide 5 (Insight 3): The counter-intuitive discovery or twist.
- Slide 6 (Practical Impact / Takeaway): The core lesson or implication.
- Slide 7 (Summary / Conclusion): Key summary and verified sources note.
- Caption: Comprehensive summary, takeaway checklist, source links, and hashtags.
